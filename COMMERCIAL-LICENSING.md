# MA2A Commercial Licensing Model — v0.1

This document explains the intended commercial boundary. It is not itself a commercial contract.

## 1. Separate assets

MA2A separates four economic assets:

1. **Protocol/specification** — public technical documentation and interoperability definitions.
2. **Official implementation** — MA2A reference and production software distributed by the project.
3. **MA2A Official Trust** — official root trust, organization credentials, compatibility/certification status, revocation and related identity services.
4. **MA2A Official Infrastructure** — optional official directories, relays, consensus/synchronization services and managed operations.

Public availability of the protocol does not automatically grant commercial rights in the copyrighted official implementation, trademark/certification rights, or admission to the MA2A Official Network.

## 2. Research and education

Non-commercial academic, educational, reproducibility and evaluation use is governed by the repository `LICENSE`.

## 3. Commercial products

Commercial products or services that incorporate the official MA2A/Memoria.ia licensed technology require a separate written commercial agreement where applicable.

The intended economic principle is that royalties, when used, are associated with the **licensed product or service incorporating the technology**, not with unrelated company-wide revenue or profit.

A commercial agreement may therefore define a base such as net revenue attributable to an identified Licensed Product, unit/OEM volume, a fixed subscription, infrastructure consumption, or a negotiated combination.

No universal royalty percentage is fixed by this public document. Commercial rates are contract-specific and may vary by scale, support level, OEM volume, infrastructure use, territory and strategic relationship.

## 4. Internal enterprise use

Enterprise internal use may be licensed through subscription, support/SLA, certification and infrastructure terms rather than a product royalty where the technology is not being sold or distributed as part of a commercial Licensed Product.

## 5. Organizational identity

The intended scalable trust hierarchy is:

```text
MA2A Official Root
        |
   Organization
   Memoria.ia / CA
    /    |     \
 agent  robot   IoT
```

The commercial relationship is primarily with the organization. The organization can manage subordinate device/agent credentials according to the active policy instead of requiring a separate commercial contract for every endpoint.

## 6. Official-network admission

Official-network admission is a separate operational authorization. A cryptographically valid certificate does not override a non-ACTIVE organization or device status.

Official-network credentials are not a DRM mechanism for preventing independent experimentation with public protocol materials. They identify membership in the official trust domain.

## 7. OEM and high-volume deployments

OEM agreements may use volume-adjusted rates, minimum commitments, support/certification obligations, or strategic terms. High endpoint count does not necessarily imply a per-device monthly infrastructure fee; deployments may aggregate through an organizational Memoria.ia authority.

## 8. Trademarks and certification

Commercial use of the names `MA2A`, `Memoria.ia`, official compatibility marks, certification labels, or representations of official-network membership is governed separately from source-code access.

## 9. Legal review

Final customer contracts, royalty definitions, tax treatment, trademark terms, audit rights and jurisdiction-specific enforceability require professional legal/accounting review before production commercialization.
